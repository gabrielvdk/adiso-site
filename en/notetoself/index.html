How it works

Fast. Really fast.

Open it and start typing. The first line becomes the subject. One tap on send and the app is gone.

From any app

Share an article, link or photo via Share → Note to Self. A link's title and summary come along automatically.

Personal and work

Pick the inbox for every note. Tags like [TODO] and [READ] keep your inbox sorted at a glance.

Always reliable

No signal? Your note waits and sends itself as soon as you're back online.

Your privacy stays yours

Note to Self sends through your own Gmail account and only asks permission to send; it can't read your email. No account with us, no servers of our own, no tracking, no ads.
